Our Story & Hospitality

Welcome to Cafe De-Light

Nestled on Saguna Khagaul Road (Balaji Nagar, Danapur), Cafe De-Light was founded in 2022 to provide young food lovers, college students, working professionals, and families with a warm, air-conditioned, and pocket-friendly sanctuary.

Whether you crave spicy street-style steamed momos with fiery red chutney, richly layered chicken dum biryani, sizzling wok-tossed Hakka noodles, or handcrafted hazelnut cold coffees, every dish at Cafe De-Light is prepared fresh to order using quality spices and ingredients.
