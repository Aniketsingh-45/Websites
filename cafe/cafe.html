About Cafe Skylite

Where every meal meets the sky.

Cafe Skylite is a premium rooftop lounge and dining destination located on the 6th floor of KV Complex, inside the Silkhouse Premier Hotel in Patna. Experience the breathtaking rooftop ambience, perfect for celebrations, romantic dinners, and unwinding under the stars.

Our philosophy: A luxurious culinary journey. We serve exquisite Indian, Chinese, Continental, North Indian, and Mughlai delicacies. Our full-service bar, live music, and karaoke nights ensure an unforgettable experience.
