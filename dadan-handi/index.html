Heritage & Craft

The 4 Sacred Secrets of Ahuna Mutton

Ahuna (or Handi Mutton) is not just a dish—it is an ancient culinary alchemy native to Champaran, Bihar.

01

The Clay Earthen Pot (मिट्टी की हांडी)

Special earthen handis crafted from the fine alluvial clay of the Ganges, cured overnight with pure mustard oil to ensure heat retention and earthy minerality.

Cured 12 Hours
02

Whole Garlic Pods & Mustard Oil

Tender mutton pieces marinated with whole unpeeled garlic bulbs (लहसुन गांठी), cold-pressed kachi ghani mustard oil, onions, and hand-pounded whole spices. Zero drop of water added.

100% Waterless
03

Wheat Dough Airtight Seal (दम सील)

The earthen lid is firmly pressed and hermetically sealed using kneaded wheat flour dough (aata seal). The high-pressure steam builds internally, tenderizing the meat in its own juices.

Dum Pukht Sealed
04

Charcoal Wood-Fire Simmer

The handi is placed on an open angithi bed of glowing charcoal and slow-simmered for 2.5 hours. Master cooks rhythmically rotate each handi by hand every 15 minutes to guarantee even cooking.

2.5h Charcoal Roasted

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

What makes Dadan Handi Ahuna Mutton different from ordinary curry?

Unlike regular restaurant curries that are boiled or fried in metal pans, Ahuna Mutton is cooked strictly inside unglazed earthen clay pots sealed with kneaded dough (dum pukht) over charcoal wood-fire. No water is added—the meat simmers for 2.5 hours purely in its natural juices, sliced onions, cold-pressed mustard oil, and whole roasted garlic pods.

Can I get the mutton delivered in the authentic clay handi?

Yes! For orders of 500g (Half Handi) and 1 Kg (Full Handi), we deliver the meal sealed inside the actual earthen clay pot so you can break the dough seal fresh at your dining table. For single combos, food is packed hot in premium food-grade containers.

What are the opening hours and peak batch times?

Our branches open at 11:00 AM and serve hot handis until 11:00 PM daily. The fresh morning handi batch comes off the wood fire by 12:30 PM, and the evening batch is ready by 7:30 PM. We recommend calling ahead for bulk 1 Kg orders!

Do you offer vegetarian options cooked in clay pots?

Yes, we prepare our signature Ahuna Paneer Handi (₹160) cooked in brand-new, dedicated vegetarian clay pots with the same traditional mustard oil and roasted spice marinade.
