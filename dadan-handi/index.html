Our Story

The Magic of
Wood Fire & Clay

Dadan Handi Meat is famous for authentic Champaran-style mutton, a traditional Bihar cooking method passed down through generations.

The meat is marinated in a rich blend of mustard oil, ghee, garlic, onions, and ginger, then sealed inside an earthen clay pot with a dough of kneaded flour, and slow-cooked for hours over a wood fire.
