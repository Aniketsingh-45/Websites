The 4-Part Daily English Formula

The proven language acceleration cycle extracted directly from page 2 of your 30-Day Upgrade protocol.

🎧
PART 01

Listening (10–20 Min)

Listen to an English tech podcast, documentary, or interview daily. First pass with English subtitles, second pass without subtitles to train pure acoustic comprehension.

Recommended: Lex Fridman, Huberman Lab, or Andrej Karpathy.
🗣️
PART 02

Shadowing Technique

Listen to native audio, wait 1 second, and repeat the sentence immediately aloud. Do not merely copy words—replicate pitch, pauses, cadence, and emotional emphasis.

Builds natural oral muscle memory without conscious translation.
📖
PART 03

Read Aloud (1–2 Pages)

Read technical articles or mindset essays aloud daily. Progression: 1st read slow for articulation → 2nd read normal speed → 3rd read expressive with vocal command.

Use the Reading Practice Room above to practice with WPM timer!
🧠
PART 04

Thinking in English

Eliminate native-language translation lag. When walking, making coffee, or debugging code, mentally narrate your actions in simple English sentences.

Shifts your default neural monologue into English fluency.

3 Quick Eye Exercises for Screen Relief:

1. 20-Foot Gaze

Look out a window or at the furthest wall in the room to relax ciliary eye muscles.

2. Slow Blinks

Blink fully 10 times slowly. Screen users blink 66% less often, causing dry eyes.

3. Palming

Rub hands together until warm and gently cup over closed eyes for 15 seconds.
