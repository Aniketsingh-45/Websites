DAILY FLUENCY PROTOCOL

The 4-Part Daily English Fluency Formula

Never study English like an academic exam. Treat it like a musical instrument or sports reflex.

PART 1 (15 MINS)

Active Native Listening

Immerse your ears in natural native speech podcasts (Lex Fridman, Huberman Lab, 3Blue1Brown) without subtitles.

PART 2 (15 MINS)

Shadowing Technique

Speak simultaneously with the speaker with 0.5s delay. Match their voice pitch, vocal cadence, emphasis, and breath pauses.

PART 3 (10 MINS)

Read Aloud Practice

Read 1–2 pages of high-quality engineering or philosophy texts out loud. Over-articulate every syllable to build muscle memory.

PART 4 (ALL DAY)

Thinking in English

Stop translating thoughts from your native language. When debugging code, formulate your inner monologue directly in English.
